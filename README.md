# PRESSPAGE.NEWS

## AI-Powered Robot Journalist News Distribution Network

PRESSPAGE.NEWS is an independent news platform powered by a Robot Journalist system that discovers, curates, analyzes, rewrites, and redistributes publicly available news and press releases into concise, readable, and accessible news stories.

The platform combines automated content collection, artificial intelligence, editorial scoring, and human oversight to provide readers with timely information while reducing misinformation, sensationalism, and information overload.

---

## Mission

Our mission is to make news more accessible by:

- Aggregating publicly available news sources.
- Converting complex information into reader\-friendly articles.
- Identifying emerging trends and breaking stories.
- Providing transparent attribution to original sources.
- Supporting independent journalism through automation and open technologies.

---

## How It Works

### 1. News Discovery

The Robot Journalist continuously monitors:

- Press releases
- Government announcements
- Public company filings
- News feeds
- Open data sources
- Community submissions

### 2. Content Analysis

Artificial intelligence models:

- Classify topics
- Detect duplicates
- Extract key facts
- Identify entities and locations
- Score relevance and credibility

### 3. Story Generation

The system automatically:

- Summarizes source material
- Rewrites content into journalistic style
- Generates headlines
- Creates article metadata
- Tags related subjects

### 4. Distribution

Published stories are distributed through:

- PRESSPAGE.NEWS website
- RSS feeds
- Social media channels
- API integrations
- Syndication partners

## Editorial Principles

PRESSPAGE.NEWS follows these guiding principles:

### Transparency

Whenever possible, original sources are cited and linked.

### Accuracy

AI-generated content may be reviewed before publication.

### Independence

Editorial decisions are not influenced by political organizations, corporations, or advertisers.

### Responsible AI

Artificial intelligence is used as an editorial assistant and content\-generation tool. Human oversight may be applied to ensure quality and compliance.

## Disclaimer

PRESSPAGE.NEWS uses automated systems to generate and distribute content. While efforts are made to ensure accuracy, readers should verify important information using primary sources before making decisions based on published material.

Opinions expressed in source materials remain the responsibility of their original authors.
